## Plans and take rate
Maison has three plans. Free: 1 vehicle, 1 driver, 3% platform fee. Growth ($299.99/month): up to 5 vehicles, 7 drivers, analytics, 2% fee. Fleet ($399.99/month): unlimited vehicles and drivers, analytics, property support, 1% fee.
Change plan under Settings, Subscription (/tenant/settings/plans). A tier change is confirmed on Stripe's own screen with the prorated amount.
If a payment fails the account gets a grace period (past due) before features are blocked.

## Stripe payouts
Connect Stripe under Settings, Business Profile, or follow the Stripe integration guide (/tenant/settings/help/stripe). Card payments and driver payouts need completed Stripe onboarding.
If onboarding is incomplete, Stripe shows what is missing; finish it via the return link from the Business Profile page.

## Troubleshooting
If a rider cannot pay by card, check Stripe onboarding and that card is allowed in Booking Settings. If a booking cannot be assigned, check the driver is active and approved and the vehicle is free at that time (a vehicle or driver cannot be double booked). See Common issues (/tenant/settings/help/troubleshooting).
