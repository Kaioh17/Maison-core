## Dashboard navigation
The tenant dashboard has a left sidebar (a bottom tab bar plus a drawer on mobile) with these pages.
Overview (/tenant/overview): revenue, ride counts, pending rides, 7-day charts, driver presence and today's bookings.
Drivers (/tenant/drivers): invite, approve, activate and assign drivers to vehicles. Riders (/tenant/riders): list of riders who signed up under your slug.
Bookings (/tenant/bookings): every booking with filters for status, service type and vehicle; assign a driver, confirm or cancel. Vehicles (/tenant/vehicles): your fleet, images, status and driver assignment.
Settings (/tenant/settings/general) and Feedback (/tenant/feedback) complete the sidebar.

## Settings pages
Account (/tenant/settings/account): your login, name, phone and password. Business Profile (/tenant/settings/company): company name, address, Stripe payouts status.
Booking Settings (/tenant/settings/tenant-settings): guest bookings, deposits per service type, allowed payment methods (card, Zelle, cash), Zelle details.
Pricing (/tenant/settings/pricing): base rates and per-mile / per-hour pricing. Vehicle Classes (/tenant/settings/vehicle-config): vehicle categories and their rates.
Branding (/tenant/settings/branding): logo, colors, font and button radius that riders see on your white-label site.
Feedback Forms (/tenant/settings/feedback-forms): link a Google Form for rider and driver feedback. Subscription (/tenant/settings/plans): change plan or manage billing.
Help (/tenant/settings/help): Operator guide, Common issues and Stripe integration guides.
